## Why I write this blog

I treat this as a long-running personal archive: it records the technology I am learning, the projects I have worked on, the books I have read, and the questions I have not figured out yet.

Writing is how I organize things. A lot of ideas are only vague shapes in my head; once they become sentences, I can see where I have not actually thought them through. This site does not chase trends or try to be a complete tutorial library. It is closer to a public notebook: a place for the things worth keeping.

## What I am doing

I focus mainly on frontend engineering and visualization, and I like turning abstract data and rules into something you can see and play with. I also write about tools, reading, and everyday thinking.

The blog itself is a long-term project too: from an early static site, to adding visual editing with TinaCMS, to redesigning the homepage and the reading experience. The stack and the design keep changing, but one thing has not: making complex things easier to explain.

## This site

- Framework: Next.js App Router + TypeScript
- Content: TinaCMS; posts and pages can be edited visually at `/admin/index.html`
- Styling: Tailwind CSS with a hand-written Neo-Brutalist / glass visual language
- Deployment: Vercel, fully static prerendering, with content updates on demand
